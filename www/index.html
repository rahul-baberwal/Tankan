Scored the way the commission scores

Every word you type is lined up against the passage. A wrong, missing or extra word is a full mistake. A word that differs only in capital letters or punctuation is half a mistake.

  • Net speed drops by every mistake, not only the final percentage
  • Data-entry tests are judged on key depressions per hour
  • A word you were halfway through when time ended is not counted against you
Net WPM = (characters ÷ 5 − full mistakes − half mistakes ÷ 2) ÷ minutes
Key depressions per hour = key presses ÷ minutes × 60

The same two formulas appear on the practice sites we studied. Your exam notice has the last word.

The railway test locks Backspace, so the RRB presets lock it too. When you are learning, switch on live colouring and an on-screen keyboard that shows the next key and the finger to use.

Hindi follows your keyboard
